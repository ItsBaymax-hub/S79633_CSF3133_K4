Top Destinations in Terengganu

Redang Island

Tucked away in the South China Sea off the coast of Terengganu, Pulau Redang is a tropical island paradise where crystal-clear water, soft white beaches, and thriving coral reefs await. Snorkel alongside colourful fish, gentle green turtles, and blacktip reef sharks in the sheltered bays of Redang Marine Park, then unwind on the golden sands of Pasir Panjang or explore the island’s quiet fishing villages. Just a short ferry ride from Kuala Terengganu or Merang, Redang is one of Southeast Asia’s most beautiful and unspoiled island getaways, best visited between March and October when the sea is calm and visibility is at its best.

Perhentian Island

The Perhentian Islands are a small group of islands off the coast of northeastern Malaysia. They are known for their crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches, and vibrant marine life. The islands are a popular destination for snorkeling, diving, and relaxing on the beach. Visitors can explore the coral reefs, swim with turtles, and enjoy the laid-back atmosphere of the islands. The Perhentians are accessible by boat from the mainland and offer a range of accommodations, from budget-friendly hostels to luxury resorts.

Visit historical sites, traditional markets, and local villages to experience the unique customs and traditions of the region. Learn about the history of the sultanate, explore ancient temples, and interact with local artisans who showcase their traditional crafts.
